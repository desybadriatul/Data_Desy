## Page 19
Market & Competitive Intelligence
Telecommunication – TELKOMSEL
Problem
TELKOMSEL had trouble measuring their digital initiatives, product (reload
voucher) performance on marketplaces, and finding out what their customers
think about various product and service categories on a weekly basis.
Solution
dataxet:sonar provided an industry overview of the telecommunication
industry in Indonesia, covering all major Telco’s and campaigns run by these
Telco's on over 25 major cities in Indonesia. In addition, dataxet:sonar provided
weekly and monthly reports on competitor movement, strengths and
weaknesses and issues related each competitor compared to TELKOMSEL’s
own services.
In addition, dataxet:sonar’s weekly reports also provided insight into each
telco’s product (reload vouchers) movement on Tokopedia and Shopee as
main the marketplaces in Indonesia. We compared pricing, shipping, product
views and customer feedback on each product, isolated stores that sold the
most vouchers and measure customer feedback on each store and
categorized them by location.
All information and analysis was delivered weekly, with execution time of within
1-2 days from cut-off. This rapid execution time was attributable to various
data automation and intelligence algorithms developed by dataxet:sonar.

Public Sector - Departemen Keuangan RI
Problem
The Indonesian Ministry of Finance has difficulty tracking negative
responses of netizens on social media related to financial issues &
policies in Indonesia, especially if it touches on governmental
institutions in Indonesia.
Solution
Sonar Platform provides key daily conversations and negative issues
related to the Ministry’s policies and officials in the form of insights
reports and 30 minutes alerts of suspected upcoming trends related to
such policies and the macro financial sector in general related to State
Assets & Debt, State Budget (APBN), Taxation, Customs & Excise, Ministry
Events and others. Which later becomes an early warning of negative
issues in monitoring social media.

Automotive - Astra Honda Motors
Problem
AHM was struggling to maintain their position in digital opposed to their
competitors and were under pressure by upper management in handling
and containing negative issues related to their products before they
managed to go viral.
Solution
Sonar Platform provided a digital benchmarking system through its
flagship product Sonar Analytics to compare and analyse prominent
social media channels and viral topics that can potentially be used for
content creation and traffic generation. Sonar also delivers top negative
posts and trend alerts through various channels such as email and
