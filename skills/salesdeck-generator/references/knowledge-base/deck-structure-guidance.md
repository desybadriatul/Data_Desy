# LIST OF PROMPTS

## Sales Deck Drafting Prompt:
General Sales Deck Structure for Reference
### 1. **Cover Slide: Media Intelligence Reimagined**
- **Purpose:** Sets the tone and grabs attention.
- **Description:** Your company logo, a compelling image, and a brief, catchy headline that hints at the solution to the client's problem.
### 2. **Dataxet Introduction**
- **Purpose:** Introduces your company and its expertise.
- **Description:** "Dataxet is the fastest-growing media intelligence group in Asia. dataxet:sonar, a part of Dataxet Group, offers real-time insights and actionable information from social, digital, and traditional sources."
### 3. **Solution Offered by Dataxet**
- **Purpose:** Delves deeper into how Dataxet addresses the challenges.
- **Description:** "We help address the challenges of a dynamic, fast-moving market" followed by the notes you provided.
### 4-14. **Benefits & Features**
- **Purpose:** Highlights the value your solution provides.
- **Description:**
- **Focus:** How to focus on what matters?
- **Audience:** How to engage the audience in the attention economy?
- **Insight:** How to distill big data into accurate, relevant insights?
- **Trend:** How to consistently identify and leverage trends relevant to your brand and customers?
- **Crisis:** How to identify and mitigate a crisis?
- **Structure for Social Success:** How to optimize social media campaigns & activations?
### 15. **Unique Selling Proposition (USP)**
- **Purpose:** Differentiates you from competitors.
- **Description:** "Why Industry Leaders Choose dataxet:sonar" followed by the points you provided.
### 16. **Testimonials**
- **Purpose:** Offers social proof.
- **Description:** Quotes or endorsements from satisfied clients. Use real names and photos (with permission) for authenticity.
### 17-21. **Tailored Solutions for Market Leading Media Intelligence**
- **Purpose:** Showcases the adaptability and customization of your solutions.
- **Description:** Breakdown of tailored solutions like brand perception and management research, and research benchmarks.
### 17. **Thank You/Contact Information**
- **Purpose:** Wraps up the presentation professionally.
- **Description:** Express gratitude for their time and provide clear ways they can get in touch.
### 18. **Backup Slides (Optional)**
- **Purpose:** Provides additional information if needed.
