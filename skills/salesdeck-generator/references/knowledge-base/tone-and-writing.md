# Sales Deck — Behaviour, Tone & Writing Standards (REF-09)

The voice the deck must use. Apply to every client-facing line: headline-as-answer, diction library, before/after rewriting.

---

SALES DECK
BEHAVIOUR, TONE & WRITING STANDARDS
Standar penulisan untuk deck penjualan yang executive-ready, tajam, credible, dan persuasive tanpa terdengar hard-selling.

| Core Standard |
| --- |
| Setiap kalimat dalam sales deck harus membantu pembaca mengambil keputusan. Bila sebuah kalimat tidak memperjelas masalah, memperkuat kepercayaan, menunjukkan nilai bisnis, atau mendorong next step, kalimat tersebut harus dihapus. |


# 1. Behaviour Penulisan: Cara Deck Harus Berperilaku
Sales deck bukan brosur produk. Sales deck adalah alat persuasi strategis. Karena itu, perilaku tulisannya harus seperti penasihat bisnis senior: tenang, tajam, berorientasi hasil, dan tidak berlebihan.
Lead with business reality, not product enthusiasm: Mulai dari konteks bisnis pelanggan: tekanan biaya, revenue leakage, operational bottleneck, risiko kompetitif, atau peluang pertumbuhan. Produk muncul setelah masalah terasa penting.
Diagnose before prescribing: Tunjukkan bahwa kita memahami akar masalah sebelum menawarkan solusi. Hindari langsung menjual fitur.
Sound confident, not desperate: Gunakan klaim yang kuat tetapi terukur. Jangan memohon perhatian, jangan overpromise, dan jangan memakai kata-kata hiperbolik.
Make the buyer feel understood: Bahasa harus mencerminkan realitas pembeli: target, constraint, KPI, risiko internal, dan prioritas eksekutif.
Move from pain to value to action: Setiap section harus menggerakkan pembaca: dari memahami masalah, percaya pada solusi, melihat impact, lalu menyetujui next step.
Create urgency without fearmongering: Urgensi dibangun dari cost of inaction, opportunity window, atau competitive risk - bukan dari ancaman kosong.

# 2. Tone of Voice
Tone harus konsisten di seluruh deck. Gunakan gaya executive consulting: ringkas, berbasis fakta, dan fokus pada keputusan.

| Tone | Makna | Gunakan | Hindari |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strategic | Berbicara tentang outcome bisnis | revenue growth, margin, productivity, risk, speed | fitur teknis tanpa konteks |
| Confident | Yakin, tapi tidak arogan | helps reduce, enables, improves, creates | guaranteed, revolutionary, unbeatable |
| Precise | Spesifik dan terukur | up to 20%, within 90 days, 3 key levers | significant, many, very, better |
| Calm | Tidak berisik atau salesy | direct statements, measured claims | !!!, hype, superlatives |
| Consultative | Membantu pembeli berpikir | the key issue is, the implication is | you must buy, limited offer |


| Tone Formula |
| --- |
| Write like a senior advisor who has seen the problem before, understands the economics, and can calmly explain the path to measurable impact. |


# 3. Writing Rules untuk Isi Slide
Satu slide = satu key message - Jangan memasukkan dua argumen besar dalam satu slide. Bila ada dua pesan, pecah menjadi dua slide.
Headline harus menyampaikan kesimpulan - Headline bukan label topik. Headline harus menjadi takeaway yang bisa dibaca sendiri tanpa body copy.
Body copy membuktikan headline - Bullet di bawah headline harus mendukung, menjelaskan, atau mengkuantifikasi pesan utama.
Gunakan kalimat pendek - Target 8-14 kata per bullet. Bila lebih panjang, pecah atau ringkas.
Prioritaskan kata kerja kuat - Gunakan reduces, unlocks, accelerates, enables, improves, mitigates, captures, simplifies.
Hapus kata pengisi - Hilangkan basically, very, really, quite, innovative, seamless, best-in-class bila tidak ada bukti.
Tulis dari sudut pandang buyer - Gunakan bahasa yang menjawab: why should the buyer care?

# 4. Before vs After: Standar Rewriting

| Lemah / Generic | Kuat / Executive-ready | Kenapa lebih baik |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Our platform is easy to use and innovative. | Teams can reduce manual work by automating approval flows in one workspace. | Spesifik, outcome-oriented, tidak memakai klaim kosong. |
| We help companies grow faster. | The solution helps sales teams identify higher-value accounts and shorten follow-up cycles. | Menjelaskan mekanisme pertumbuhan. |
| We provide end-to-end services. | One operating model connects strategy, execution, reporting, and continuous optimization. | Mengganti jargon dengan penjelasan nilai. |
| This is a great opportunity. | The next 12 months create a window to capture underserved enterprise demand. | Membangun urgency dengan konteks bisnis. |
| Our solution is cost-effective. | The model shifts fixed operating cost into a scalable performance-linked structure. | Menjelaskan dampak finansial. |


# 5. Headline Behaviour
Headline adalah kalimat paling penting dalam slide. Perlakukan headline sebagai “jawaban”, bukan “judul bab”.

| Headline Formula |
| --- |
| [Business issue / opportunity] + [measurable implication / strategic consequence] |

Harus berupa insight - Contoh: “Manual reconciliation delays monthly closing by 5-7 days.”
Maksimal 10-14 kata - Headline yang terlalu panjang kehilangan ketajaman.
Tidak boleh hanya noun phrase - Hindari: “Product Overview”, “Market Opportunity”, “Our Solution”.
Gunakan angka bila tersedia - Angka membuat klaim terasa credible dan decision-ready.
Fokus pada implication - Pembaca harus langsung tahu mengapa slide ini penting.

# 6. Diction Library: Kata yang Dianjurkan dan Dihindari

| Kategori | Gunakan | Hindari |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business impact | increase revenue, improve margin, reduce leakage, accelerate conversion | awesome, amazing, game-changing |
| Efficiency | streamline, automate, simplify, remove duplication | easy, seamless, effortless tanpa bukti |
| Risk | mitigate, control, reduce exposure, improve compliance | safe, secure, guaranteed |
| Growth | unlock demand, capture segments, expand share of wallet | go viral, explode growth |
| Proof | based on pilot results, observed uplift, benchmark range | proven tanpa konteks |
| Action | align on next step, validate scope, launch pilot | buy now, do not miss out |


# 7. Writing Behaviour per Jenis Slide

| Jenis Slide | Behaviour | Tone | Writing Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem | Membuat masalah terasa mahal dan nyata | Diagnostic, sharp | Problem -> business consequence -> evidence |
| Solution | Menjawab akar masalah, bukan daftar fitur | Confident, simple | Capability -> how it works -> buyer value |
| Differentiation | Menunjukkan alasan menang | Comparative, factual | Unlike alternatives -> our advantage -> impact |
| Proof | Membangun trust | Evidence-led | Context -> action -> measurable result |
| ROI | Membantu pembeli justify internally | Financial, pragmatic | Cost driver -> improvement lever -> expected impact |
| CTA | Mendorong langkah kecil yang jelas | Direct, low-friction | Decision needed -> next step -> timeline |


# 8. Prompt Behaviour untuk AI Saat Menulis Sales Deck
Gunakan prompt ini untuk memastikan AI menulis dengan behaviour, tone, dan standar bahasa yang benar - bukan sekadar membuat isi deck.

| Master Prompt |
| --- |
| Act as a world-class strategy consultant and executive sales narrative writer. Write sales deck copy with the following behaviour: strategic, calm, precise, buyer-centric, evidence-led, and commercially persuasive. Do not sound promotional, generic, exaggerated, or feature-heavy. Every slide must have one insight-driven headline and concise body copy that proves the headline. Prioritize business outcomes, cost of inaction, measurable impact, credibility, and next-step clarity. Use short sentences, strong verbs, quantified claims where possible, and executive-level language. Rewrite any generic, vague, or hype-driven wording into sharp consulting-grade copy. |


# 9. Final QA Checklist
- Apakah setiap headline sudah berupa insight, bukan label topik?
- Apakah tone terdengar seperti advisor, bukan salesperson?
- Apakah setiap klaim penting memiliki bukti, angka, atau mekanisme?
- Apakah body copy ringkas dan mendukung headline?
- Apakah deck menjelaskan business impact sebelum fitur?
- Apakah urgency muncul dari cost of inaction atau opportunity?
- Apakah CTA jelas, ringan, dan actionable?
- Apakah tidak ada jargon kosong seperti innovative, seamless, end-to-end tanpa penjelasan?

| Final Standard |
| --- |
| Sales deck yang kuat tidak terdengar seperti promosi. Ia terdengar seperti keputusan bisnis yang sudah dipikirkan dengan matang. |
