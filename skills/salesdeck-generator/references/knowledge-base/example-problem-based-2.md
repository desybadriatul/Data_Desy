## Slide 4
The Impact of Negative Publicity on Launch Success
Creating buzz is essential in transforming a new product launch from a mere event into a market sensation.
Slide 4: Problem Statement
Impact Points of Negative Publicity for Product Launch
Limited Consumer Awareness
Without buzz, potential customers may remain unaware of the new product, limiting its market penetration.
Poor Brand Perception
Buzz helps shape the narrative around a product, influencing public perception and brand image.
Market Saturation Risks
In a crowded market, negative buzz can cause a product to lose its competitive edge, making it blend into the background with other similar offerings.
Negative Buzz & Revenue Impact
When buzz turns negative, it can significantly deter sales, as potential customers may shy away from the product due to concerns and skepticism.
Brand Loyalty Erosion
Negative buzz can undermine long-term brand loyalty, deterring potential loyal customers from the outset.
