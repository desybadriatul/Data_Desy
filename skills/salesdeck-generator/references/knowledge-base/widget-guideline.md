# DXT360 Widget Guideline (REF-08)

What each dashboard widget shows, its metrics, applicable channels, and business use. Use when specifying visual_spec / render_payload and when proposing dashboard demos.

---

## Dashboard Overview
The dashboard is your central hub for monitoring and analyzing social and mainstream media data. It provides access to a range of widgets that offer insights into your brand, industry trends, and audience behavior.

### Key Features
1. Dashboard Access
Definition: Secure entry point to your personalized analytics and monitoring tools.
Purpose: To provide you with a personalized view of your social listening data.
How to Use:
Logging In: Enter your username and password on the login page.
Navigating: Use the main menu to access different sections of the platform.
Benefits:
Secure access to your data.
Customized dashboard layout.
Tips:
Regularly update your password for security.
Customize your dashboard by adding or rearranging widgets.
2. Search Keywords
Definition: Terms or phrases you input to track specific topics, brands, or campaigns.
Purpose: To focus your data collection on relevant conversations.
How to Use:
Entering Keywords: In the search bar, type the keywords or phrases you want to monitor.
Using Operators: Use Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT) to refine your search.
Saving Searches: Save frequent searches for easy access.
Benefits:
Targeted data collection.
Reduced irrelevant data ("noise").
Tips:
Be specific with keywords to improve accuracy.
Regularly review and update keywords to align with current campaigns.
3. Advanced Filter
Definition: Tools that allow you to narrow down data based on specific criteria.
Purpose: To provide precise data analysis by filtering out unnecessary information.
How to Use:
Accessing Filters: Click on the 'Advanced Filter' option.
Selecting Criteria: Choose filters like sources (social media, digital media, traditional media), language, country, gender, age group, sentiment, etc.
Applying Filters: Click 'Apply' to update your data view.
Benefits:
In-depth data analysis.
Ability to focus on specific data subsets.
Tips:
Combine multiple filters for granular insights.
Save filter settings for future use.
