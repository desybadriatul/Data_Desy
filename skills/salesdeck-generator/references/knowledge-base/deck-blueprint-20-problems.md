# Sales Deck Blueprint — 20 Problems (REF-06)

Maps common client problems to recommended deck structure. Use to anchor slide flow once the primary problem type is classified.

---

Initial Prompt → Can you please give me a formula of a great sales deck presentation for a media intelligence company that can be used to solve our client brand's common problems below:
Difficulty in generating new leads
Declining sales or revenue (sales, e-commerce)
Lack of engagement with social media content
Limited engagement with customers, community, or supporters
Declining sales or revenue (marketing, web development)
Negative publicity or reviews
High customer churn rate
Low employee morale or motivation
Limited industry recognition or influence
Need to generate buzz and interest around a new product launch
Low event attendance or engagement
Need to increase brand awareness or reach a new audience
Limited reach or engagement on social media
Negative media coverage or a crisis situation
Limited customer service channels or long wait times
Low product adoption or usage
Limited understanding of customer needs and preferences
Limited qualified candidates or low applicant volume
Need to establish or expand partnerships
Please give me the blueprint only so I can adjust it according to each problem.
GPT Answer → → Certainly, here's a generalized blueprint for a sales deck for a media intelligence company, targeted towards solving the listed client brand problems:
Slide 1: Opening
Title: Addressing Today's Brand Challenges
Subtitle: How [Your Company] Drives Solutions in the Media Landscape
Note: Briefly introduce the session and the pivotal role of media intelligence in addressing brand challenges.
Slide 2: Context and Problem Statement
Title: Navigating the Modern Media Maze
Contents: Outline key issues faced by brands (choose a specific problem like “Declining E-Commerce Sales” for this deck).
Visual: Use graphs/imagery to depict the problem, such as a declining sales graph.
Slide 3: Relevance of Media Intelligence
Title: The Power of Informed Decision-Making
Contents: Define and discuss the impact of media intelligence on brands, focusing on data-driven decisions.
Slide 4: Introducing [Your Company]
Title: Meet [Your Company]
Contents: Quick facts about your company, mission, vision, and notable achievements.
Visual: Include logos of well-known clients or key metrics to build credibility.
Slide 5: Solution Overview
Title: Our Solution to [Specific Problem]
Contents: Introduce your unique media intelligence solution aimed at solving the specific problem highlighted earlier.
Visual: Illustrate solution aspects using icons, brief texts, or images.
Slide 6: Key Features & Benefits
Title: Unlocking Success with [Your Product/Service]
Contents: Enumerate features and align them with benefits, emphasizing addressing the key problem.
Visual: Utilize icons and short texts for clarity and brevity.
Slide 7: Success Stories
Title: Proven Impact, Proven Success
Contents: Share a case study or testimonials demonstrating how your solutions have driven results.
Visual: Use graphs and quotes to visualize success.
Slide 8: Differentiation
Title: Why [Your Company]?
Contents: Highlight unique selling propositions (USPs) and differentiators in your offerings.
Visual: Consider a comparison chart or unique icons for each differentiator.
Slide 9: Pricing and Packages
Title: Invest in Your Success
Contents: Present your pricing model and packages, focusing on ROI.
Visual: Create clean, easy-to-understand pricing tables.
Slide 10: Implementation & Support
Title: Our Commitment to Your Growth
Contents: Discuss your approach to onboarding, implementation, and ongoing support.
Slide 11: Call to Action
Title: Let’s Shape the Future Together
Contents: Encourage to take the next step - schedule a demo, a discovery call, or avail a trial.
Visual: Provide contact details visibly and attractively.
Slide 12: Thank You & Q/A
Title: Thank You!
Subtitle: [Your Company] - Your Partner in Navigating Media Intelligence
Note: Allocate time for questions and provide appreciative closing remarks.
Tailoring to Specific Problems:
Always adjust the Solution Overview and Key Features & Benefits slides to focus on how your media intelligence services address the specific issue highlighted in the Context and Problem Statement.
The Success Stories should be chosen to reflect scenarios where similar problems were tackled effectively.
This sales deck provides a structured narrative while keeping it succinct and tailored to address the specific problems faced by potential client brands using your media intelligence solutions. Adjust the key slides to align with the respective problems while maintaining a coherent flow.

Slide 1: Introduction

Begin with an engaging quote or statistic about lead generation.

Slide 2: The Challenge
Title: The Lead Generation Conundrum
Visual: Graphs showing declining leads.

Transition: "We’ve encountered this roadblock, and here’s the landscape of how we navigate through it."
Slide 3: Setting the Context
Title: Identifying the Underlying Issue
Share quick facts or short stories about the modern consumer's journey, emphasizing the importance of robust lead generation strategies.
Transition: "This perspective brings us to an innovative, yet simple solution tailored for you."
Slide 4: Our Solution
Title: Your Gateway to Prospective Clients
Present a simplified overview of your solution, ensuring that it’s easy to understand and emphasizing the value it provides.
Transition with a question: "But how does this solution translate into real-world success?"
Slide 5: Success Stories
Title: From Strategy to Triumph
Share a brief yet impactful story of a client whose leads skyrocketed after implementing your solution.
Use visuals: Before/After graphs, positive reviews, etc.
Transition: "Undoubtedly, questions about the applicability may arise. Let’s delve deeper."
Slide 6: Features & Benefits
Title: Unpacking the Treasure Trove
Bullet points of key features followed by brief explanations – ensure this slide is not text-heavy.
Highlight benefits with each feature, maintaining simplicity and clarity.
Transition: "You might be wondering about the investment required to unlock these benefits."
Slide 7: Investment & ROI
Title: Your Investment, Multifold Returns
Clearly lay out pricing or investment levels.
Showcase potential ROI using relatable and tangible examples.
Transition: "Investment queries often come bundled with potential apprehensions."
Slide 8: Addressing Your Concerns
Title: Mitigating Reservations
Preemptively address common objections or concerns regarding your solution.
Transition: "Our journey doesn’t end after overcoming concerns; it merely transforms into a fruitful relationship."
Slide 9: Our Support & Partnership
Title: A Symbiotic Journey
Highlight post-purchase support, your commitment to their success, and community aspects.
Transition: "Embarking on this journey is straightforward and immediate."
Slide 10: Next Steps & CTA
Title: Paving the Path Forward
Define clear and simple steps for them to take to get started.
Highlight urgency or scarcity, if applicable.
Thank You: A warm thank you note for their time and consideration.
Slide 11: Q&A
Title: Your Insights Matter
Open the floor to questions or provide contact details for future inquiries.
